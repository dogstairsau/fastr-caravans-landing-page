# Fastr Caravan LP — Design Context

## Users
Australian caravan buyers, primarily 40–65. Near-retirees, semi-retired tradies, families planning the **Big Lap**. Median spend $60k–$120k. ~80% mobile traffic from Meta ads. Value clarity, trust, and proof of competence over visual fireworks.

## Job to be done
Land from a Meta ad → believe within 5 seconds this is real → see lender breadth + indicative repayment → get a quote in <60s without surrendering full identity → feel a real person will handle them.

## Brand personality
**Real. Confident. Aussie. Friendly.** Optimistic, not corporate.

## Aesthetic direction (revised — staging-aligned)
**Friendly editorial** — soft pastel feature spreads (mint, lavender), real caravan photography in **organic/arched containers**, brand-color illustrations layered alongside photos (clouds, mountains, falling "F" meteors), big confident display type with **single coral word-accents** for rhythm, asymmetric magazine layouts. Light primary, with full-bleed navy "feature spreads" used like magazine page-turns. Drawn from `staging.fastrfinance.com.au/caravan-loans/`.

## Typography (locked — client preference)
- **Display**: Oakes Grotesk (client supplies .woff2). Placeholder: Space Grotesk.
- **Body**: Manrope (primary) + Inter (fallback) — both already loaded via `next/font`.
- **Accent emphasis**: solo words rendered in coral (e.g., *Caravan **finance** is our thing.*) — never gradient text.

## Colors
- Navy `#0F0E2B` — primary text, dark feature spreads
- Coral `#FF485E` — primary CTA + word accents
- Cyan `#009ECB` — illustrations, secondary accents
- Yellow `#FCB400` — illustrations, micro-highlights
- **Mint** `#D5F5EC` — hero / final CTA section bg
- **Lavender** `#E2E5FB` — mid-page brand-statement section bg
- White / Sand `#FAF8F3` — photo-led sections

## Design principles
1. **Pastel section bgs** — mint / lavender / white / navy alternate like magazine spreads.
2. **Photos in organic shapes** — arched, rounded-rect, circular crops. No flat 16:9 hero rectangles.
3. **Brand illustrations** layered with photos — cyan clouds, yellow/coral "F" meteor, simple birds.
4. **Coral on a single word per heading** — rhythm device, never gradient.
5. **Asymmetric** — left text, right photo+illustration cluster. Magazine, not 4-up grid.
6. **Type does the heavy lifting** — display 80–110px on desktop, no banner highlights, no all-caps headings.
7. **Earn the chrome** — no icon-in-rounded-square cards, no border-stripes, no glassmorphism.

## Constraints
- Mobile-first (LCP <2s on 4G)
- WCAG AA on body text
- ASIC compliance footer preserved (ABN, ACR, ACL)
- Form: 2-step on LP → redirect to `/form/classic` with prefill (Option A)
